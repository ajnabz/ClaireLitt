About Claire Litt

Design that looks good and works hard

Claire Litt creates functional, well-designed spaces and visual communications for businesses. The work combines creative thinking with practical industry knowledge, helping clients present their brand clearly, use space effectively and move confidently from concept to delivery.

Projects range from exhibition environments and corporate interiors to full working drawings, display units, catalogues and sales materials.
